Privacy Policy

Pocket Bloom Games · Effective November 19, 2026

Who we are

Pocket Bloom Games is an independent game developer based in Canada. This policy explains what information our mobile games collect and how it is used. It applies to the following apps on Google Play:

  • Hexa Bloom (com.pocketbloomgames.hexabloom)

Questions? Email studio.pocketbloomgames@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in your Google account so you can restore it on another device.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to count installs and see how the game is played (for example which levels are hard). It is not used for personalised ads.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and your choices

Our games use Google AdMob. When you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from that form.

On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). You can then still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

Purchases such as "Remove Ads" or coin packs are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment information under its own privacy policy. We receive only the information needed to confirm the purchase and give you the item.

Children

Our games are intended for players aged 13 and over and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game, which deletes it. If you use Play Games cloud save, the copy in your Google account stays until you delete it from Google Play Games. We do not keep a copy ourselves. Detailed analytics data is kept for at most 14 months and then deleted automatically. Information collected by Google (ads, purchases, Play Games and analytics) is kept and deleted under Google's policies. You can manage it in your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

Security

All communication with Google services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Because we do not run our own servers or collect personal information, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how our games handle information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
